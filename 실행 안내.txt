PAWDAY - LOCAL PET CARE (WINDOWS)

1. Double-click Pawday.cmd (or 포데이 실행.cmd).
2. Wait for your browser to open. No ChatGPT account or login is needed.
3. Click Add a pet, then add schedules. The interface opens in English.

The launcher window closes automatically. Pawday keeps running in the background.
Double-click Pawday.cmd again to reopen the same app.
Double-click Stop Pawday.cmd when you want to stop the background app.

Your records are saved on THIS computer, even if you close the browser or restart Pawday.
The local database and uploaded files are in petcare/.wrangler/state.
Do not delete that folder if you want to keep your data.

Keep this entire folder together, including .runtime, petcare/node_modules and petcare/dist.
The old online shortcut is not needed for local use.
Each teammate needs their own copy of the complete app folder and their own local data directory.
Do not distribute your petcare/.wrangler/state folder with personal records; a clean copy initializes its own database.

Notifications: allow browser notifications, then use Send test now or Demo in 10 seconds.
Keep the browser running during the notification demo.
